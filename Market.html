How to play

Controls

WASDWalk. The arrow keys work too.
SpaceLift the block in front of you, or place the one you carry.
RRestart the room.
EscPause.
MMute or unmute.

Menus use the mouse. On a touch screen, an on-screen pad appears under the room.

The goal

  1. Read the clue beside each numbered pedestal in the panel next to the room.
  2. Walk up to a block, face it and press Space to lift it. The panel describes whatever you face.
  3. Face a pedestal and press Space. A correct block locks in and explains why.
  4. A wrong block floats back to where it started. Some blocks are decoys.
  5. When every pedestal is filled, the hatch opens. Walk onto it to dive deeper.

Three stars for no wrong placements, two for one or two, one for more. Every correct answer is saved in Study notes.
